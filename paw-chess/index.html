❓ How to Play Chess

♟

Pawn walks one square forward (two on its very first go) and eats one square diagonally. March it to the far end and it becomes a Queen — or a Rook, Bishop or Knight! 👑

♜

Rook slides as far as it likes in straight lines — along rows and columns.

♞

Knight is the horse! It hops in an L-shape (two + one) and is the ONLY piece that can jump over friends.

♝

Bishop slides as far as it likes along diagonals — it stays on its own color forever!

♛

Queen slides any direction — rows, columns and diagonals. She's the strongest piece!

♚

King takes one careful step in any direction. When the enemy king can't escape, that's checkmate — you win! ♟️

🎯

Tap your piece to see 🟢 dots where it can go. Stuck? The 💡 Hint button shows a good move, and ↩️ Undo takes moves back — no limits, no worries!

🌟

Chess has lots more secrets — castling, en passant and more. Ask a grown-up to show you the rest!
