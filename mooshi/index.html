❓ How to Play

🐮

This is Mooshi, the happiest little blob-cow. Mooshi is never sad, sick or lonely — and friendship only ever grows. If you go away for days, Mooshi simply naps happily waiting for you! 😴💛

👆

Move your pointer around — Mooshi's big eyes follow it! Tap Mooshi for a happy boing and a heart.

🍎

Feed: pick a snack. Mooshi waddles to the bowl and munches. If Mooshi is already full, they'll politely say so — no worries, nothing bad happens!

🎾

Play: bounce the ball (Mooshi watches and hops!), tickle by wiggling your pointer fast over Mooshi, or snuggle up for a nap.

🫧

Clean: soap bubbles rain down for a few seconds — tap them to pop! It ends with a sparkly shine. ✨

🎀

Dress-up: as your friendship level grows you unlock hats and extras — party hat, bow tie, flower, glasses, scarf, beanie and a crown! 👑

❤️

Every kind thing you do adds friendship. It never goes down, ever. The sky in the window even matches the real time of day! 🌅🌙

⌨️

Space = boing poke. P or Esc = pause. No rush — Mooshi is happy whenever you visit. 💛

Variables

A variable is a named number. boing decides how high Mooshi hops when you poke. Slide it up — sproing!

Timers & loops

The game loop ticks about 60 times a second. Every snackTimer × 40 seconds of play, Mooshi's tummy slides down 1 point. Lots of tiny ticks = smooth wiggles!

🤔
Conditions (IF)

Computers love IF questions. IF wiggle is big, Mooshi breathes and wiggles faster; IF Mooshi is napping, it slows to a cozy sway. Try wiggle = 2!
