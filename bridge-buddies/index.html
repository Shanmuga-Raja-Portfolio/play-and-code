❓ How to Play

🔩

Smiling bolts are stuck in the rocks. Tap one bolt, then tap another bolt nearby — a wooden plank snaps in between!

📏

The dashed line shows how far your plank can reach. Too far? Pick closer bolts, or use a LONG plank (the one with golden bands)!

↩️

Tap a plank to take it back — it returns to your plank box. Use 🧹 Clear all to start fresh.

▶️

Press Go buddy! and your friend walks across your bridge to the flag. If the path stops, they'll shrug and come back — just add more bridge!

⭐

Use par planks or fewer for 3 stars. Fewer planks, more stars!

🏝️

Finish all 12 levels to unlock the Sandbox — unlimited planks, endless bridges!

💡

Rock pillars are free walking! Two bolts on the same pillar top? Your buddy just strolls across it.

Variables

A variable is a named number. plankReach decides how long your planks can stretch — slide it and watch the dashed reach circle grow!

🔢
Numbers & math

Every plank droops by bridgeSag. Set it to 0 and bridges stay perfectly straight; set it to 2 and they dip like a noodle. Math is a superpower!

🔁
Loops

The game loop runs about 60 times a second. Each run moves your buddy a tiny step using buddySpeed — lots of tiny steps = smooth walking!
