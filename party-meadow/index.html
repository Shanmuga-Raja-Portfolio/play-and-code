❓ How to Play

👫 Take turns on this PC with your family! You share one meadow — try to beat each other's best scores!
🌼

Pick a game from the meadow. Each flower sign shows your best stars. Earn ⭐ to grow your Meadow Stars!

🐰

Bunny Pops — tap bunnies when they pop out of their burrows (+1). Don't tap the buzzy bees (−2)!

🦉

Memory Grove — flip two cards at a time and remember where the animal friends hide. Fewer moves = more stars!

🌸

Flower Song — watch the flowers sing their tune, then tap the same tune back. It grows one note every round!

🍎

Fruit Catch — move the basket with ← → (or A / D, or the big buttons) and catch fruit. Dodge the boots!

🔍

Odd One Out — find the one emoji that is different. The board grows bigger every round — go quick for stars!

🎈

Balloon Pop — pop the floating balloons (+1). Golden balloons are worth +3 but float away fast!

⏸️

Press P or Esc to pause any game. Turn sound on or off in Settings.

Variables

A variable is a named number. bunnyPace decides how fast new bunnies pop out of their burrows — slide it up and the meadow turns into a bunny storm!

🔢
Numbers & multiplication

Your basket slides fruitSpeed × 430 pixels every second. Set fruitSpeed to 2 and your basket zooms twice as fast!

🔁
Loops

Balloon Pop runs its loop 60 times a second, and each run spawns new balloons using balloonPace. A loop doing tiny steps, over and over, fills the sky!

⏱️
Timing

Flower Song waits 460 ms between notes. songTempo divides that wait — set it to 2 and the flowers sing twice as fast. Tiny waits add up to a whole tune!

⌨️ Type real code!

Try bunnyPace = 2, or fruitSpeed = 1 + 1, or even balloonPace = bunnyPace * 2 — then press Enter.
