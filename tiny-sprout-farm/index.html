❓ How to Play

🌱

Tap an empty brown square and pick a seed. Grandpa Sprout left you two carrot seeds to start!

💧

When you see a droplet 💧, tap the square (or the little 🚿) to water it. Un-watered plants simply wait — they never wilt!

🥕

When a plant sparkles and bounces, it's ripe — tap it to harvest! Coins go straight into your piggy bank. 🪙

🛒

Buy new seeds, funny decorations and more garden plots in the Shop. Prices never change — no surprises!

📖

Your first harvest of each crop earns a golden sticker. Fill the album to win the Golden Watering Can ✨🚿!

💛

Plants only grow while you're looking at the farm, and they wait patiently when you leave. No rush, ever — happy farming!

Variables

A variable is a number with a name. 4 times every second the game runs a tiny loop that visits all 24 plots and grows each plant a little × growSpeed. Slide it and watch the farm speed up!

🔢
Numbers

A carrot needs 2 drinks of water. waterPower is how many drinks one 🚿 tap gives — set it to 2 and a single tap fills a carrot right up!

✖️
Multiplication

Every harvest pays the sell price × coinBonus. A 🥕 sells for 12 🪙, so coinBonus = 2 pays 24 🪙. Math is a superpower!

⌨️ Type real code!

Try growSpeed = 2, or coinBonus = 1 + 1, or even growSpeed = waterPower * 2 — then press Enter.
