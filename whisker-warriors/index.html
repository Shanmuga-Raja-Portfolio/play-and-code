❓ How to Play

🐟

Fish power fills up all by itself — it's your deploy money. A bigger Wallet means more fish, faster!

🐱

Tap a cat card (or press keys 1-7) to send that hero marching to the right. Each cat costs fish and needs a short cat-nap (cooldown) before going again.

⚔️

Heroes fight any dogs they meet. Archers and mages shoot from far away; guards are super tough walls; Big Tabby just stomps!

🏰

Guard your Cat Tower on the left. Smash the Dog Base on the right to win the stage!

⭐

Win stars (keep your tower healthy for 3⭐!) and coins. Replaying stages you already beat still earns coins.

🎓

Spend coins at Cat College to level up heroes, your wallet and your tower. Every upgrade always works — no surprises!

💡

Stuck on a stage? Bring more Pawsome Guards to block, and level up at the College!

Every hit does damage × heroDmg. Set it to 2 and your heroes hit twice as hard. Math is a superpower!

Loops

The game loop runs about 60 times a second. Each run moves every hero a tiny step using heroSpeed — lots of tiny steps = smooth walking!
