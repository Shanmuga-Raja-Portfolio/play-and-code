❓ How to Play

🎮

Press ↑ to go, ↓ to brake or back up, ← → to steer. On a tablet, use the big round buttons. (W, A, S, D and Space work too!)

🏁

Race 3 laps around the track. The little arrow above your kart is you — beat the other animal racers to the finish line!

💨

Drive over the glowing boost pads for a super-speed zoom. Wheee!

🌱

Stay on the dirt track! Driving on the grass makes your kart slow and sleepy — but you can never get stuck.

↩️

If you see "Wrong way!", turn around and follow the track the other way.

⭐

Finish 1st for 3⭐ (and to unlock the next track and a new kart color), 2nd for 2⭐, 3rd or 4th for 1⭐. Your best time is saved!

🎨

Change your animal racer and kart color anytime from the menu.

Variables

A variable is a named number. MAXS is your kart's top speed — slide it and feel what 265 pixels per second really means!

🔁
Loops

The game loop runs ~60 times every second. Each run pushes your kart forward using ACC (acceleration) for a tiny moment — lots of tiny pushes = zoom!

❓
Conditions (if)

The code asks if your kart left the track, and only then slows it by GRASS (0.44 = keep 44% of your speed). If-statements make the rules!

⌨️ Type real code!

Try MAXS = 300, or STEER = 1 + 1, or even GRASS = MAXS / 10 — then press Enter.
