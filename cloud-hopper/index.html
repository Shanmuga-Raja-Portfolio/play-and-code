❓ How to Play

🐰

You are a fluffy sky bunny! Hop across the islands and reach the baby animal waiting in its nest.

🎮

Move with ← → (or A/D), jump with Space, ↑ or W. Or just tap the big buttons under the game!

🍓

Every level hides 3 fruits. All 3 = 3⭐, two = 2⭐, finishing = 1⭐. Fruits are also hat money!

🚩

Touch a flag to save your spot. Prickly bushes only make you poof back to the flag — no worries, try again!

🍄

Land on a bouncy mushroom for a SUPER-duper jump. Hold ➡️ while you fly!

☁️

Cloud platforms are soft — you can jump up through them and land on top.

🌬️

Some levels are windy. The arrow at the top shows which way the wind blows.

🎩

Spend fruits in the Hat Shop — crowns, bows, party hats! Wear any hat you own, any time.

Variables

A variable is a named number. gravity decides how fast the bunny falls — slide it down and you float like a balloon!

🔢
Numbers & math

jumpPower is how hard the bunny kicks off. Height loves squares: ×1.5 power ≈ ×2.25 height. Math is a superpower!

🔁
Loops

The game loop runs about 60 times a second. Each run adds a little gravity and moves the bunny a tiny step — lots of tiny steps = smooth hopping!
