❓ How to Play

🖌️

Free Draw: pick a color (or the 🌈 rainbow brush — it sparkles!) and draw with your finger or mouse. Add emoji stamps, change the paper, undo oopsies, then 💾 save your doodle to the Gallery.

🎨

Coloring: pick a cute page, pick a color, then tap any part of the picture to fill it in. Tap again with a new color to change it, or reset the page to start fresh.

🖼️

Gallery: see all your saved doodles. Tap one to see it big, ⬇️ keep it as a picture file on the computer, or 🗑️ say bye-bye (tap delete twice — just in case!).

🛠️

Game Master Lab: be the boss of the app! Move sliders to change the brush, the rainbow and the stamps — and try typing real code, like brushSize = 2.

💛

No scores, no losing, no waiting — just happy drawing. Every doodle is perfect!

Variables

A variable is a named number. brushSize multiplies how fat your brush is — set it to 3 and draw a GIANT line!

⚡
Events & functions

Every tap on the paper is an event that runs a tiny recipe called a function. The stamp recipe reads stampSize to pick the emoji size — the same recipe, bigger result!

🔁
Loops

The 🌈 rainbow brush loops around the color wheel, stepping the hue a little every bit of your stroke. rainbowSpeed is the step size — big steps zoom through the rainbow!
